The distribution is frozen on purpose. A real model re-runs the whole network for every token, and the next distribution depends on the token just sampled — which is why one bad draw can send a sentence somewhere it never recovers from.

Why this matters

Sampling is the difference between a model that always says the same thing and a model you can use. Temperature zero gives you the argmax, which is deterministic and prone to loops; a long tail gives you variety and nonsense. Top-k caps the tail by rank, top-p caps it by accumulated probability, and min-p caps it relative to the best candidate, so the cutoff adapts when the model is unsure. Every setting is a trade between coherence and surprise, and none of them changes what the model knows — only which of its guesses you see.
