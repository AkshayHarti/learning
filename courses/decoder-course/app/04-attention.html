3 · The shape of one head

Softmax runs across the key axis, independently for every query row. The scaling by √d keeps the logits from growing with dimension, which would saturate the softmax and kill the gradient.

Why this matters

Attention is the only place where tokens exchange information, so almost every capability traceable to context — coreference, syntax, copying a name from the prompt — is attention finding the right row and column. Temperature and the causal mask are not cosmetic: a mask bug during training lets the model read the token it is supposed to predict, and the loss collapses while the model learns nothing useful. The softmax row is also where "attention" gets its interpretability claims, and where those claims break: a high weight means information flowed, not that the model "understood" the relationship.

Concept: scaled dot-product attention, softmax, causal masking.
